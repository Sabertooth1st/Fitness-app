What stays on your phone

Without an account, your plan, workouts, settings, XP and exercise familiarity are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. The optional gym location you save is stored only on your device and is never uploaded or used for analytics.

Location

ARRIVE reads your location only when you tap “Check with my location”, once, while the app is open. It is compared on your device to your saved gym location. ARRIVE does not use background location and keeps no location history. A check-in records only the method (self-reported or location-assisted) and the time.

Optional cloud backup

If you create an account and agree to backup, we store your email address, training records (sessions, sets, effort answers), preferences, XP and milestones with [[UNRESOLVED: hosting provider and region, e.g. Supabase project region]]. Training records can be health-related personal data; we process them only to provide the service you asked for.

What we do not do

No advertising SDKs, no selling of data, no session recordings, and no sharing of your fitness data with AI services. Analytics are off unless you opt in, and never include pain details, coordinates, tokens, email addresses or free-text health notes.
